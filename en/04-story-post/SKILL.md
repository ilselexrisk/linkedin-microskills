# LinkedIn Story Post

## Why this skill exists
The top B2B creators agree that stories beat lectures. Dave Gerhardt says it took him years to learn that people want to hear what happened to you, not be taught. Lara Acosta built her following on the **SLAY** structure: Story → Lesson → Actionable → You.

This skill adds a fifth step, **Bridge to offer**, because the goal here is leads, not just likes.

## Inputs (ask if missing, max 3 questions)
- **The raw story:** what happened? Who was involved, what was at stake, what did you do, what happened next? Bullet points are fine.
- **ICP card** or at least: who the post is for.
- **Offer / next step:** what a reader who relates should do (optional).

## Never invent the story
If the user gives no real experience, ask for one. Don't make up anecdotes, clients or numbers. Fake stories get noticed, and then trust is gone.

## The arc
1. **Story (40%)** — start mid-scene. Give one specific moment with concrete details (time, place, number, what someone said). Show the problem; don't explain it. Keep it to 3–6 short lines.
2. **Lesson (15%)** — the single insight, in one or two lines. It should be something the ICP feels too.
3. **Actionable (30%)** — 3–5 steps the reader can apply this week, using "→" or numbers. This is where the expertise shows.
4. **You (10%)** — turn it to the reader: a specific question about *their* situation (not "thoughts?").
5. **Bridge to offer (5%, optional)** — one soft line: "If [ICP pain] sounds familiar, [low-friction next step]."

## Signposting
Somewhere in the story or lesson, show how you know this, e.g. "after 40 client audits…". Jasmin Alić's point: experts share knowledge but forget to show where it came from.

## Process
1. Pull out the **turning point**: the moment something changed.
2. Draft the arc above.
3. Run it through `linkedin-hook-writer` patterns #2 (Story drop-in) and #1 (Dense credibility) to get 3 hook options.
4. Pass to `linkedin-post-formatter` rules: short paragraphs, no links in body, one CTA.
5. Deliver the post with 3 hook options and a recommended one.

## Output format
```
RECOMMENDED HOOK: …
ALT HOOKS: 1) … 2) …

[FINAL POST]

---
Arc check: Story ✓ Lesson ✓ Actionable ✓ You ✓ Bridge ✓/–
Lead path: [what a qualified reader does next]
```

## Don'ts
- No "I was broke and crying on the floor → now I'm rich" melodrama unless it really happened, and even then tone it down.
- No humblebrags dressed up as lessons.
- No more than one CTA.
